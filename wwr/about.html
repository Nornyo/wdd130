Rapid Rush Adventures

Our purpose is to bring adventure, joy, and connection to every person who joins us on the river. We believe the best memories are made with a paddle in your hand, friends beside you, and rapids ahead. Our motto: Ride the river, love the adventure!

History

Rapid Rush Adventures began in 2010 with one raft, three friends, and a love for wild rivers. What started as weekend trips for friends quickly grew into a full rafting company.

Today, we guide hundreds of adventurers every year down some of the most beautiful rivers, while staying true to our roots: safety first, fun always, and respect for nature.
